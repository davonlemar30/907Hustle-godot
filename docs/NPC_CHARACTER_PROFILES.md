# 907Hustle NPC Character Profiles

This is the working visual-reference layer for the portrait pass. Established
facts come from the ClickUp asset checklist and the Godot dialogue, job,
relationship, and system data. Details marked as visual direction are grounded
in the role and are not new gameplay canon.

## NPCs

### Mina Vale

- Role: Night Owl counter clerk; possible romance; careful observer.
- Canon cues: works evenings, notices regulars and neighborhood talk, speaks
  briefly, offers the player work, and values discretion over bravado.
- Portrait direction: adult Black woman, practical work apron and dark layers,
  watchful eyes, guarded warmth, maintainable hair; no glamour styling.

### Dre Smooth

- Role: lender and later junior-lender relationship through The Book.
- Canon cues: always closing, financially exacting, rewards honesty and follow-
  through, and presents terms rather than visible numeric trust.
- Portrait direction: adult Black man, neat understated coat and knitwear,
  composed sales confidence, polished but not flashy.

### Biniam Tesfaye

- Role: Ethiopian owner and host of The Nile; gambling and neutral-ground venue.
- Canon cues: his space is a social room where information travels without
  making him a faction leader.
- Portrait direction: Ethiopian man, mature and measured, practical dark wool
  layers, hospitable but unreadable host's expression.

### Selam Tesfaye

- Role: Biniam's Ethiopian sister; wellness-spa owner and intelligence source.
- Canon cues: connected to The Nile household while operating a quieter,
  observant service business.
- Portrait direction: Ethiopian woman, composed and perceptive, neat low-
  maintenance grooming, dark practical professional layers.

### Reggie

- Role: Mountain View barbershop owner, community figure, and neighborhood
  gatekeeper.
- Canon cues: asks who the player is with; represents community access rather
  than criminal hierarchy.
- Portrait direction: adult Black man, mature, solid working build, barbershop-
  practical jacket/flannel layers, patient but assessing authority.

## Job managers

### Lani

- Role: older Samoan owner of Wash & Go.
- Canon cues: calls everybody “baby,” pays daily cash, is warm without being
  soft, and notices whether people show up.
- Portrait direction: older Samoan woman, silver threaded hair, work apron,
  lined face, direct affectionate no-nonsense expression.

### Marcus

- Role: quiet night manager at the Spenard Chevron.
- Canon cues: tight ship, few words, controls keys and schedule, quietly looks
  after people who prove reliable.
- Portrait direction: adult Black man, lean practical build, station-work
  jacket, reserved watchful expression.

### Sonny

- Role: owner of the Rebel; job manager and car connection through his nephew.
- Canon cues: checks hands before faces, values competence and honesty, has a
  family-run store and a dry sense of humor.
- Portrait direction: older adult Black man, stocky working build, weathered
  store-owner layers, skeptical but fair expression.

### Denise

- Role: retail floor lead and job manager.
- Canon cues: manages floor, freight, breaks, and customer problems; practical,
  rule-aware, and humane in small ways.
- Portrait direction: adult Black woman, simple work vest and natural grooming,
  focused matter-of-fact face with restrained kindness.

### Ray

- Role: dock foreman and job manager.
- Canon cues: works the port, starts at six, tests a handshake, trusts Juan's
  recommendation, and expects people not to lose the crew.
- Portrait direction: adult Black man, sturdy dock-worker build, weathered
  winter workwear, firm operational expression.

### Big Mike

- Role: freight job manager.
- Canon cues: controls routes and manifests, is known by the name “Big Mike,”
  and gives practical responsibility without ceremony.
- Portrait direction: large-built adult Black man, heavy winter freight layers,
  calm intimidating presence, plain direct expression.

## Existing-canon reconciliation notes

The ClickUp checklist does not provide full biographies for every manager or
secondary NPC. The profiles above intentionally stay within role, dialogue,
relationships, location, and gameplay function already present in the Godot
repository. They are portrait-production guidance, not new mechanics.
